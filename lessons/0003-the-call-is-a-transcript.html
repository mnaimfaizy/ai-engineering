3. Pick — safe or spicy?

The model lines up guesses for the next token (lesson 0001). Temperature is how boldly it rolls the dice. Low = always the favourite. High = sometimes a wild guess. OpenAI’s knob runs from 0 to 2. temperature

Low (safe)

“What is 2 + 2?” → four, every time. Invoices, JSON, facts.

High (spicy)

“Name a pizza” → surprise pies. Stories. Not for a price list.

Finish: “I want to eat ___”

Four made-up guesses. Drag toward 0 (safe) or 2 (spicy). Watch which bar stays the winner.
