From lesson 0013. Encode the valid paths. Leave holes where an agent may wander.

  1. NodeDoes work. Code, one LLM call, or a full agent.

Not

  • Not the opposite of a loop — a loop is a tiny cyclic graph. Loop card.

  • Workflow vs agent is still the first split. That card.
