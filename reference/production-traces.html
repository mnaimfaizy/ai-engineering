Not

  • Not three log files with three ids. That is not a path.
  • Not a vendor APM. OpenTelemetry is the interchange.
  • Not an eval suite — that samples these trees next. Eval suites. Floor: evals.
  • Not default full-prompt capture in prod. Opt-in debug.

Who mints

  • The SDK at the first hop with no traceparent — not the LLM.
  • Context lives in RAM for the request; spans go to your backend; Stripe/SQL only if you copy the id.
  • The agent inherits the bag only if your harness keeps it (in-process or inject on HTTP).
